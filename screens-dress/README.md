# screens-dress

Interfaces layer. The Dress stage.

- Its inspector: layers, the armed tool's options, the garment with its fit, areas, edges, holes and hem ink, tuck, loose ends and color.
- The Library drawer's Garments half: outfits, garments by slot and their menus.
- The Paint tool that paints a garment's areas on the body in a brush or a shape, Edit curves, Erase and Smooth on their edges, Hole, and the press tools that pick a garment and hang a loose end.

**Uses:** `screens-core`, never another `screens-*` module.
**Used by:** `app`.
