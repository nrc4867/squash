# screens-dress

Interfaces layer. The Dress stage.

- Its inspector: layers, the garment with its fit, edges and hem ink, tuck, loose ends and color.
- The Library drawer's Garments half: outfits, garments by slot and their menus.
- The Edge and Hole tools that draw a garment on the body, and the press tools that pick a garment and hang a loose end.

**Uses:** `screens-core`, never another `screens-*` module.
**Used by:** `app`.
