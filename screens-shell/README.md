# screens-shell

Interfaces layer. The app's outer screens and the Build workspace's chrome.

- Home and New character.
- The Build frame and its chrome. The workspace and the tools' drawer sections come in as a `BuildContent` from `app`.
- The Library drawer and its Parts half, and the Parts tree.

**Uses:** `screens-core`, never another `screens-*` module.
**Used by:** `app`.
