# Boards

The state of every screen against its board in [Squash flow of work](https://claude.ai/artifact/KfBGmYcUEuoRJuhvGky3CT). States: planned, building, verifiable, verified. A session sets building and verifiable and fills in Verify; only Nicholas sets verified. The board's note in the artifact carries the same state.

Verify is what a person runs to see the board working: a run configuration or Gradle task, then the actions to take, then what must be true.

The Build workspace is being restructured to the boards in [Squash flow of work · restructure](https://claude.ai/artifact/81F1TgKZKUdhUVPzKZs9zJ): four stages, one inspector, and drawers that only show and hide. Its rows are under Build, restructured. Until a restructured board is built, the screen it replaces follows its board in Squash flow of work.

## Decisions

- **Locality.** Moving an outline's far end, a span holding its lowest or highest x, or a Front section outline may move every ring, since the ring count is fixed. See HLD: data-model, Refits are local.
- **Legs that lean.** When a leg leans, the other legs shorten to match and the body drops, so all paws stay on the ground. A lean changes no stored length, so leaning back restores every leg. See HLD: data-model, Placing parts.
