# Frames

Generated by `generateSchemas` from the part builder and the rig. For any built recipe, `tools/recipe.sh probe` answers the same questions on its own parts.

## Body space

| axis | points |
| --- | --- |
| +x | forward, where the character faces |
| +y | up |
| -z | the character's left; +z its right |
| origin | the root part's socket end; the ground is under the lowest point |

A pair is described as its left side; its right side mirrors it across z = 0, and reads v as 1 - v.
`reference=` compares use another frame: metres, +y up, the character facing +z with its left at +x.
A face place `[x, y]` (regions, marks and eyes by `face`) is the head seen from straight ahead: x to the character's left, negative for its right, y up, in the head's drawn units from its socket.

## Part frame

By the direction a part rests in (a template's rest, or a recipe's `rest`), which way its outlines' y and its v run, at u 0.5. x of Side and Top runs along the part from its socket end. Front draws the section in -1 to 1: its x is Top's -y, its y Side's +y.

| rests | Side's +y | Top's +y | v 0 faces | v 0.25 faces | v 0.5 faces | v 0.75 faces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| forward | up | right | up | left | down | right |
| back | up | left | up | right | down | left |
| up | forward | left | forward | right | back | left |
| down | forward | right | forward | left | back | right |
| out | up | forward | up | back | down | forward |

## Bone axes

A pose bone `{"part", "joint", "degrees", "lean", "turn", "twist"}` on a part's joint, by the part's rest and the joint's hinge (a template joint's hinge is in templates.md; a recipe joint's is its `hinge`, x when left out). Each cell is where +30 of that angle alone moves the bone's tip, or for `turn` and `twist`, which only roll it, where its Side's +y goes. Negative angles move the other way. The right side of a pair takes the same numbers as a mirror image.

| rests | hinge | +degrees: tip | +lean: tip | +turn: up | +twist: up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| forward | x | down | left | right | right |
| forward | y | left | up | right | right |
| forward | z | stays | left | back | back |
| back | x | down | right | left | left |
| back | y | right | up | left | left |
| back | z | stays | right | forward | forward |
| up | x | back | right | left | left |
| up | y | right | forward | left | left |
| up | z | stays | right | down | down |
| down | x | back | left | right | right |
| down | y | left | forward | right | right |
| down | z | stays | left | up | up |
| out | x | down | back | forward | forward |
| out | y | back | up | forward | forward |
| out | z | stays | up | stays | stays |

Worked example: a leg resting down, its `hip` on hinge x. `{"part": "leg-l", "joint": "hip", "degrees": 30}` swings its foot back; `"lean": 30` swings it left, and on `leg-r` the same lean swings it the mirror way. A knee or elbow bends by `degrees` within its limits; `mode: "free"` lifts the limits. `twist` rolls a bone spread along its length (a forearm from elbow to wrist); `turn` rolls it whole.
