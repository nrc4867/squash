# Templates

Every template at its newest version, which a recipe part gets when it gives no `version`. Directions are the character's own: forward, back, up, down, left, right (see frames.md). `u` runs 0 at the socket end to 1 at the tip; `v` runs round the part. Lengths and radii are body units at `size` 1. Generated by `generateSchemas` from `templates/`.
